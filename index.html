Strapd

All your wearables in one dashboard.

Beta. Strapd is in a private, invite-only beta for adults in the United States (Android first).

Your watch, ring, sleep tracker and health apps each tell part of the story. Strapd puts sleep, heart rate, HRV, steps, calories and workouts side by side, shows where each number came from, and tells you how today compares with your own normal.

What it connects

On your Android phone, through Health Connect

Anything that shares with Health Connect, including Samsung Health (Galaxy Watch and Ring), Google Health (Fitbit and Pixel Watch), Garmin Connect, Oura and WHOOP. Read-only: Strapd never writes to Health Connect.

Brand accounts (rolling out, starting with Polar)

Sign in directly with Polar, Oura, WHOOP or Withings to add the metrics they don't share with Health Connect, such as brand sleep scores, readiness and recovery. Garmin works through Health Connect.

Coming later

iPhone and Apple Health. Sweat and hydration trackers (Nix, Gatorade Gx) are listed on the dashboard as devices to discover.

Private by design

  • No ads, and your health data is never sold.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • Health Connect data is read on your phone and stays there.
  • Disconnect any device or clear the app's data on your phone from inside the app; ask us by email to delete anything on our server.

Strapd is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
